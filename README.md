# PokeNotebook

A notebook for Pokémon Champions VGC doubles, for Windows. Build and check your teams, track battles turn by turn, and see how each team is doing: lead pairs, the Pokémon you face most, and lessons to act on. Everything stays on your PC.

## Download

**[Get the latest version](../../releases/latest)**, then under **Assets** pick one:

- **`PokeNotebook-Setup-<version>.exe` (recommended).** Run it to install. No admin password needed. It adds PokeNotebook to the Start menu, and the app keeps itself up to date.
- **`PokeNotebook-<version>-portable.zip`.** Unzip it anywhere and run `PokeNotebook.exe`. Nothing is installed. The app will tell you when there's a new version, but you download it yourself.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

### "Windows protected your PC"

PokeNotebook isn't code-signed (that needs a paid certificate), so the first time you run it Windows SmartScreen may show this warning. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Updates

The installed version checks for updates once a day when it starts. When there's a new one, it shows what changed and offers **Install and restart**. It closes, updates and opens again in under a minute. You can turn the check off, or check right away, in **Settings → Updates**. The check only asks GitHub for the newest release; nothing about you or your data is sent.

## Your data

Your teams, battles and notes are saved on your PC in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PokeNotebook`. Updating or uninstalling never touches them. Settings shows where they are and lets you choose another folder.

To share a team with someone, use **Copy as paste** in the team editor; they paste it into **Import paste**.
